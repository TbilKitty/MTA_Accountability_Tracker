Recordkeeping & evidence standards

Each manually entered incident begins as Unverified rider report. Verification requires independent evidence such as matching vehicle and trip updates, dispatch records, or corroborated documentation. A report can remain inconclusive. This demonstration does not calculate delay durations or assign legal conclusions.
