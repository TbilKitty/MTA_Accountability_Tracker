School commute report · every MTA local bus route

Are Baltimore's buses getting students to school?

On every school day, this project records where every MTA local bus is, every 30 seconds, through the morning and afternoon school commutes. It then compares what each bus actually did with the published schedule. No student is tracked; results are reported by route and stop.

Definitions and limits

What counts as on time
MTA's own standard: from 2 minutes early to 7 minutes late, measured at scheduled timepoints.

What this data can and cannot show. Arrival times come from MTA's own real-time feed, recorded every 30 seconds, so a single time can be off by up to about 30 seconds. Times less certain than 3 minutes are left out of the on-time figures. A trip with no real-time record is very likely a missed trip, but a bus with broken tracking equipment would look the same, so the two are reported separately from trips MTA itself marked canceled. Periods when the feed was down are marked "not monitored" and excluded. GPS cannot show whether a bus passed a waiting student without stopping; pass-ups need rider reports. Only school days on the Baltimore City Public Schools calendar are counted, and afternoon results skip early-dismissal days.
